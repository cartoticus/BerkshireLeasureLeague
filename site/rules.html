Official Rules

Nine-hole weekly rounds. USGA Rules of Golf govern play except where modified by the Local Rules below. Two formats are scored every week from the same round: Individual Stroke Play and Two-Player Best Ball.

Local Rules

Breakfast Balls

Each player may take up to two (2) breakfast balls per round (i.e., per week).

  • A breakfast ball is a free replay of a shot, taken without penalty and without counting the original stroke.
  • Must be announced to the group before playing the replay shot ("taking a breakfast ball").
  • Played from the same spot as the original shot — tee shots only, unless the group agrees otherwise before the round.
  • The original ball is abandoned; only the replay shot and everything after it counts toward the score.
  • Maximum two per player per round — no carryover to future rounds if unused.
  • Best-ball format: each partner has their own two breakfast balls; not shared or pooled between teammates.

Gimmes

Putts inside the leather (approx. 18 inches, the length of the grip on a putter, laid flat from ball to hole) may be conceded by any player in the group and holed out as scored.

Course Selection

Rounds are logged through 18Birdies as flexible-window Virtual tournaments, so there's no automatic course lock. Every player is responsible for manually selecting the correct week's course before starting their round.

Standard Rules (USGA, as applied)

Lost Ball / Out of Bounds — Stroke and Distance

If a ball is lost (not found within three minutes of starting to look) or hit out of bounds, the player must return to the spot of the previous shot and replay it, adding one penalty stroke. A provisional ball may be played immediately after the original to save time.

Unplayable Lie

A player may declare their own ball unplayable anywhere on the course (except a water hazard) and choose one of three options, each with a one-stroke penalty:

  1. Stroke and distance — replay from the previous spot.
  2. Drop within two club-lengths of the unplayable spot, no closer to the hole.
  3. Drop any distance behind the unplayable spot, keeping that spot between the hole and the drop.

Water Hazards / Penalty Areas

If a ball is hit into a marked penalty area and not played as it lies, the player takes a one-stroke penalty and may replay from the previous spot, drop behind the hazard keeping the entry point in line with the hole, or (for a lateral hazard) drop within two club-lengths of where the ball crossed the margin.

Order of Play

In stroke play, the player farthest from the hole plays first ("away"). Ready golf is encouraged in this league to keep pace of play reasonable.

Scoring: Individual Stroke Play uses gross score for the round. In Best Ball, the team's score on each hole is the lower of the two partners' scores; the round total is the sum of those best-ball hole scores. Weekly finish points come from the 18Birdies leaderboard, with ties sharing points and skipping the next slot.
