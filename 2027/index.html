Same format, same fun

One nine-hole round per week on a flexible 7-day window, scored two ways every time: Individual Stroke Play and Two-Player Best Ball.

Finish points every week build toward season-long individual and team standings, with prize funds paid out at season's end. See the rules page for the full dues and payout structure.

House rules included

Standard USGA rules apply, plus league favorites like two breakfast balls per player per round. Full rule set on the rules page.
